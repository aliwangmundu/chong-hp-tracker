Chong HP Tracker

A minimal HP and AC tracker for Owlbear Rodeo.

Add it to a room with this manifest URL:

…/manifest.json
  • Every token on the map shows up in the list automatically.
  • Two categories: Players and Adversaries. Drag rows between them.
  • Type -25, +8, or -25 + 8 straight into the HP field.
  • HP bar and AC bubble render on the token.
